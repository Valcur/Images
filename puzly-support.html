Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resume a puzzle I've already started?
Open Puzly and go to the "My Puzzles" tab. Any puzzle you've started will show a progress bar — tap it to pick up right where you left off. You can also resume directly from the banner at the top of the home screen.

How does the color filter work?
While playing, tap the filter icon to reveal color swatches drawn from the puzzle's own picture. Tap a color to show only the pieces that match it, making it easier to hunt down a specific part of the image. There's also a filter to show only border pieces.

Can I change how many pieces a puzzle has?
Yes. When you start a puzzle, you'll be asked to choose a piece count — pick an easier, faster puzzle or a longer, more detailed one.

Do pieces need to be rotated the right way to lock into place?
By default, pieces snap into place automatically once positioned correctly, regardless of rotation. If you'd rather rotate pieces by hand, you can turn this off in Settings.

Is Puzly free?
Yes, Puzly is free to play with no ads.
