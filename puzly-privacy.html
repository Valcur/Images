Privacy Policy

Puzly · Last updated: 15 August 2026

Puzly is designed to work without collecting your personal data. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

None. Puzly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, and we have no access to it:

  • Your progress on each puzzle (piece positions and completion status)
  • Which puzzle folders you have added to your library
  • Your app settings (language, display preferences)

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

Network access

Puzly connects to the internet only to download puzzle images and the puzzle catalogue. These requests are made over a secure connection and are not associated with any identity. We do not log or profile individual users.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

Puzly contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or any third-party tracking technology.

Third parties

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

Puzly is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
